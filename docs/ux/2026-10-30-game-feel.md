# Game Feel review: welcome screen and quest editor (2026-10-30)

## 1. Verdict

The world is right: the mist, the castle, the toucan landing and the gilded buttons already feel like a storybook. What breaks the spell is underneath. The welcome card is a stack of three little forms, each with its own Save, and the payoff (+XP, the bar filling) plays while the card is still coming out of the mist. The quest editor reads like a settings page: plain checkboxes, a "0 XP" chip, four footer buttons, and ten loose options under "Advanced settings". Fix three things: **one action commits everything**, **rewards land when the player can see them**, and **every option sits next to what it changes**.

## 2. Top problems (ranked)

1. **Typed words are lost on the way out.** "Complete check-in" only runs the check-in's submitter (`submitCheckin`, `WelcomeCard.tsx:111,393`). A priority or intention typed without pressing its own Save is thrown away by `leave()`. Three Save buttons and two submit buttons ("Submit check-in" in `EnergyCheck.tsx:644`, plus the footer) compete for the same job.
2. **The reward plays to an empty room.** The card fades in from 0.5 s and is only fully visible at about 1.5 s (`mist-emerge`, `globals.css:1099`). The XP count-up runs 0.35–1.05 s (`WelcomeCard.tsx:138`), and `pc-xp` and `pc-fill` start at 0.3 s. On a phone the reward also sits under the fold, below the streak, the line and the bar: four rows (about 190 px) for one moment of praise.
3. **The editor's footers are crowded.** Create shows Cancel, Create quest (greyed and desaturated until there's a title) and Already done ✓. Edit shows Delete quest, ✓ Saved, Complete and Done. × and autosave already cover Cancel and Done.
4. **Options sit far from what they change.**
   - "Not actionable yet" is a scheduling idea but sits beside the matrix button.
   - "Stands for…" and "Also on" (a native `<select>`) are board options, but they sit away from the Board chip (`QuestEditorDialog.tsx:1921-1975`).
   - "Add tasks" hides inside the Time panel, and it is named "tasks" when the section is "Steps".
   - In arrange mode, "First visit of the day, then again after [−5 hrs+]" wraps and leaves "away" alone on its own line (`WelcomeArrange.tsx:56-67`).
5. **The facts row breaks its own rules.**
   - "Additional" alone means nothing.
   - "0 XP" is a zero counter.
   - On a phone the "No board" chip wraps onto a second line.
   - Tiny task shows up twice in the + sheet: once as a tab (`CreateSheet.tsx:21`) and again as the editor's 🌱 toolbar button (`QuestEditorDialog.tsx:967-985`).
6. **Arrange mode doubles every heading.** "YOUR TOP PRIORITY ✎" sits above "What are your top priorities today?", and "TODAY IS ABOUT… ✎" above "Today is about…" (`WelcomeArrange.tsx:266-271`). The page also has three Done controls: the panel's Done, the bottom Done and the gear's ✓.
7. **Folds snap shut.**
   - Panels open with `.reveal`, but they close by unmounting, so whatever is below them jumps.
   - The Advanced and fact-panel heights never animate.
   - The welcome exit dive takes 2.4 s every single day, and there's no way to skip it.
8. **Small inconsistencies.**
   - "Energy" is capitalised and "mood" is not.
   - Each check-in row reserves an empty 58 px column on its right.
   - The editor still uses native checkboxes for Tiny, Paint a portrait, Private and Stands for, though new work is meant to use `GoldCheck`.
   - The four scales take four rows (about 176 px) of faint icons.

## 3. Redesign

### Welcome screen, phone (390 px)

```
      (toucan) "Shall we plan your day together?"         (⚙)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│          Huzzah! Here you are!            ✎    │  greeting, font-display 26
│ ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ 🔥 Day 8  ·  ✦ +10 XP  ·  ◉ +2             │ │  reward ribbon, one line
│ │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░  Lv 4 → 5             │ │  bar 8px inside the ribbon
│ └────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│              [ ☀ Check in ▾ ]                  │  unchanged gilded button
│ What are your top priorities today?            │
│ ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ What is most important now?                │ │
│ └──────────[ + Other priorities ]────────────┘ │  seal on the border (keep)
│ Today is about…                                │
│ [ e.g. Steady and kind to myself           ]   │  no Save button
│      Breathe. Then the next small thing.  👍👎 │
│              [ ⏳ 3 due soon ]                 │
│ [☾ Snooze]                  [ Set forth  › ]   │  44px, the one commit
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Reward ribbon.** When the streak, bar and reward parts are neighbours in the order, they render as one parchment ribbon (h ≈ 56 px), the way `LevelScales` groups neighbouring scales. It goes under the greeting so it's seen first, and its motion starts once the card has settled, at 1.55 s:
  - ribbon `scale .9→1` over 260 ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1.4)`
  - XP counts up over 600 ms
  - the bar fills over 700 ms
  - confetti fires from the ribbon at 2.0 s
- **Set forth ›** is the single exit. It commits the check-in, the priority and the intention, in that order. Each one stamps a small gold ✓ seal beside its field (`scale 1.6→1`, 220 ms, staggered 90 ms). Then comes the dive. Before anything is entered the label is the same; there's no "Complete"/"Continue" swap.
- **Enter** in the intention, or ⌘↵ anywhere, also commits that field with its seal, so nothing ever needs a Save button.
- **The check-in, opened.** Energy and Mood each take one row: label (sentence case), slider, and a value bubble on the thumb. The reserved column and the inner "Submit check-in" go. "+ Tag / event" and "+ Sleep & wake" share one line.
- **The exit dive** shortens to 1.4 s, and any tap jumps to Today.

### Welcome settings mode

- The parts keep their look. Each gets a 1.5 px dashed gold outline (`rgba(139,112,64,.45)`, radius 12) and no label row.
- **✎ sits inline after the part's own heading text.** Tapping a question rewrites it in place, the same way the greeting already works, so the field-in-a-popover for questions goes away.
- The hide eye sits in the top-right corner (as now). A hidden part shrinks to a 32 px faded strip with an open eye.
- On drag pickup the part lifts: `scale 1.02`, a shadow, and `rotate(-0.6deg)` over 120 ms.
- The panel above reads: `Welcome screen ............ 🎉 Confetti` and then `Welcome me back after [ − 5 hrs + ] away` (or "Only the day's first visit" at 0).
- **Done:** only the gear's ✓ plus one sticky Done at the card's bottom. The panel's Done goes.

### Quest editor, phone (create)

```
┌ New quest                    📜 Templates   ×  ┐
│ ┌──────┐  [ Quest title                     ]  │
│ │  ✓   │  [ Description (optional)          ]  │
│ └──────┘                                       │
│ ◇ Paint a portrait   ◇ Tiny                    │  GoldChecks, one line
│ ┌─────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐ │  ledger, 2×2, h-12 cells
│ │ SHOWS IN            │ TIME                 │ │
│ │ 📍 Additional ▾     │ ⏱ 30 min ▾           │ │
│ ├─────────────────────┼──────────────────────┤ │
│ │ REWARD              │ BOARD                │ │
│ │ ✦ 30 XP · ◉ 3 ▾     │ ▦ None ▾             │ │
│ └─────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘ │
│  ↳ the open cell's panel drops under its row   │
│ ☰ Steps                       + Add a step     │
│ # health  # learning  [+ tag]                  │
│ ▸ More   Importance 2 · Urgency 3              │  summary, not "6 more"
├────────────────────────────────────────────────┤  sticky footer
│ Already done ✓             [ Create quest ✦30 ]│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The ledger.** Each cell has small caps (11 px, gold `#8a6116`) above an ink value in 15 px semibold. The reward cell reads "✦ XP" until there's a value, never "0 XP". When the time sets the XP, the number ticks up with a 150 ms pop.
- **Time panel.** Tokens: `5 · 15 · 30 · 60 · 90 · Other` (h-9 pills, the chosen one gilded). Under them sits `📅 Schedule…`. The scheduling block now includes "◇ Not actionable yet".
- **Board panel.** The picker, then "◇ Stands for its current assignment", then "Also on: [chips] + board" (a parchment popover, not a native select).
- **More.** The four scales open as `LevelScales` bars side by side (one row, about 96 px tall). Under them, a quiet "🎯 Plot on the Priority Matrix" link.
- **Footer (create).** "Already done ✓" is a quiet text button on the left. The primary button stays gold at 60% until there's a title (no desaturation). Cancel goes, since × and the leave toast cover it. ⌘↵ submits.

### Quest editor (edit)

- The header reads `Edit quest  🔒  ⋯  ×`.
  - 🔒 is the Private toggle (pressed means gold wash).
  - ⋯ also opens on right-click, and holds 📜 Save as a template, Delete quest (with an inline confirm) and Undo completion.
- Progress comes out of the fold and becomes a slim ribbon under the description: an 8 px bar, a "20%" bubble, and "4 of 20 XP earned" in 12 px.
- **Footer.** `[ ✓ Complete +16 XP ]` (gold, primary, right) and `Done` (quiet, left). The title's green check is the only save indicator, so the "✓ Saved" text goes.
- **On Complete.** The editor's check mark does the existing `qc-checkDrop` stamp (500 ms version). Then the card sinks into its section (the existing shrink path, `QuestEditorDialog.tsx:704`).

### Motion system (applies everywhere)

| Moment | Motion |
|---|---|
| Fold open/close (all) | `grid-template-rows 0fr↔1fr` 220 ms `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`; content opacity 160 ms; caret rotates 180 ms. Closing animates too. |
| Popover/panel from a chip | `scaleY .92→1` + fade, 180 ms, origin at the chip |
| Button press | `scale .96` 80 ms, release 160 ms with overshoot |
| Saved | gold seal ✓ `scale 1.6→1` 220 ms |
| Reward | ribbon pop 260 ms, count-up 600 ms, bar 700 ms, all after the card settles |
| Reduced motion | fades only, 120 ms |

## 4. Implementation list (ordered, each shippable)

1. **One commit on the welcome screen.** In `WelcomeCard.tsx`, `TopPriority` and `Intention` register submitters, the way `submitCheckin` does. "Set forth" awaits all three, then `leave()`. Remove both Save buttons and add the seal stamp.
2. **Reward timing.** Add a `--pc-delay` variable to `.pc-xp` and `.pc-fill` in `globals.css`. `WelcomeCard` sets it to 1.55 s and delays its count-up by the same amount. Confetti fires from the ribbon at 2.0 s.
3. **Reward ribbon.** Render neighbouring `streak`, `bar` and `reward` parts as one ribbon. The default order puts it after the greeting (`lib/welcome.ts`).
4. **Exit.** Shorten the dive to 1.4 s and let a tap skip it (`MistScene.tsx`, the `leave()` timeout).
5. **Check-in in simple mode** (`EnergyCheck.tsx`): drop the 58 px column and the inner Submit, show labels in sentence case, and put the value bubble on the thumb.
6. **Arrange mode** (`WelcomeArrange.tsx` `Part`): remove the label row, move ✎ after the heading, edit questions in place, collapse hidden parts to a strip, use one Done, and use the new "Welcome me back after…" copy.
7. **Shared `<Fold open>`** in `components/ui/Fold.tsx` plus a `.fold` class. Use it for the editor's Advanced section, fact panels, Sequence and Scheduling, for Other priorities, and for the check-in.
8. **Editor footers** (`QuestEditorDialog.tsx:2443-2537`), as specified above.
9. **Editor header.** Add the 🔒 toggle and the ⋯ menu (Delete, Save as template, Undo completion).
10. **Facts ledger.** Build the 2×2 grid with time tokens. Move "Add a step" to Steps and the Not-actionable checkbox into Scheduling. Move Stands for and Also on into the Board panel.
11. **Scales and More.** Default the editor's scales to bars, and make the More header summarise the values that are set.
12. **Checkboxes.** Replace every native checkbox in the editor with `GoldCheck`.
13. **CreateSheet.** Drop the 🌱 toolbar button when the sheet has a Tiny tab. Tiny becomes the GoldCheck under the portrait in both create and edit.
14. **Layout default.** Bump `quest-editor-layout.ts` to v3: progress unfolds by default, and stored layouts migrate.
15. **NightFields.** One heading. Effort chips get a gold edge and hindrance chips a rust edge, in one cloud. A tapped chip pops (160 ms). "Edit the lists" becomes a ✎ beside the heading.

## 5. What NOT to change

- The mist, the castle, the birds, the toucan landing, and his menu.
- The gilded Check in button that opens in place.
- The "+ Other priorities" seal sitting on the box's border.
- Dragging the part itself in arrange mode (no grips, no arrows).
- Autosave with the green check on the title, the leave toast with "Save as draft", ⌘↵ and ⌘S, and × at the top right.
- XP following the minutes until it's set by hand.
- The finished quest card shrinking into its section.
- The Due soon drawer's real cards and the z-49 step-down.
- The quest-complete `qc-*` celebration (it is the app's best moment; reuse it, don't dilute it).
