## What the app is

A personal goal and quest app with an adventure / parchment look, built for me (Nathan, "Lord Bob II") and a few friends.
Live at goal-dashboard-kappa.vercel.app.

- **Quests** are tasks. Each quest is worth XP and coins, and quests level you up and defeat "bosses".
- **Today screen:** top priorities, Additional Quests, daily habits, evening rites, an energy check-in and today's victories.
- **Boards and folders** group quests, for example by project or area of life.
- **Calendar:** day, 3-day, week and month views, with Google Calendar events alongside the quests I've scheduled.
  - The gear menu lets me toggle each Google calendar on and off, plus a one-tap "Google calendars" switch.
  - Quests can show as portrait cards.
  - A **scheduling list** sits beside the calendar: tap a quest, then tap a time to place it.
- **Insights:** check-in trends, an event and #tag log, and a **life wheel**.
  - The life wheel is a radar chart rating areas 1–10: Health, Relationships, Career, Money, Fun, Growth, Home, Spirit.
  - The areas can be edited.
- **Extras:**
  - secret missions (long-term quests with steps);
  - a summit "adventure path" view of today;
  - a Great Toucan Spirit companion that offers wisdom;
  - in-app feedback.

## Just built: planning rituals

**Weekly ritual (+30 XP):**
1. Look back: what I finished, what went well, what got in the way, one thing to try.
2. Mind sweep with GTD trigger prompts.
3. Sort each item: Schedule / Quest / Waiting for / Someday / Did it / Drop.
4. Review overdue and stale quests.
5. Pick 3 big rocks.
6. Place them on the calendar.
7. Seal.

**Monthly ritual (+60 XP):**
1. Look back.
2. Life wheel.
3. Pick up to 3 life-wheel areas to focus on, plus up to 3 outcomes.
4. Sweep and sort.
5. Someday review.
6. Key dates.
7. Seal.

A nudge appears on Today on Sundays and Mondays, and on the 1st–3rd of the month.

## What I want next (to think through)

### 1. Life wheel as the backbone of the app

- The life wheel areas *are* the top level of my folder and board structure.
- Main areas, like Family, subdivide into their components (e.g. Family → partner, kids, parents, siblings).
- In the rituals I choose which areas to focus on.
- This needs an elegant interface for viewing and editing the area → sub-area → board tree.

Questions to talk through:
- What are my main areas, and what are the sub-areas of each?
- Should a quest belong to exactly one area, or can it touch several?
- How deep should the tree go: area → sub-area → board → quest, or simpler?
- Should the wheel's 1–10 rating be per main area only, or per sub-area too?
- What happens to my existing boards: do I map them into areas once, by hand?

### 2. Stronger support for specific goals

What does "advanced goal support" mean to me? Possibilities:
- measurable targets (run 3×/week, save $X);
- milestones and deadlines;
- progress tracking over time;
- a goal → weekly actions breakdown;
- check-ins on whether I'm on track;
- linking goals to life-wheel areas and monthly outcomes.

Which of these matter most, and what do I want to see each week?

### 3. Fitting everything into the calendar

This is the hardest part of both rituals.

- **Free-time finder:** each week and month, find open times I could offer friends to hang out, and good times for hosting events.
- **Cross-check my other calendars:** when a candidate time is found, check my many other calendars for anything I should know about before committing.
- **Option A:** quick toggles for each calendar.
- **Option B:** pick which calendars to cross-reference.
- **Showing conflicts:** conflicts appear visually, so I can decide between the new plan and the thing already in that calendar.

Questions to talk through:
- Which calendars count as "busy" versus "just FYI"?
- What makes a good hangout time versus a good hosting time (evenings, weekends, length, buffer, travel)?
- Should it produce a shareable list of times to text friends?
- Should it fill in time for big rocks and goals automatically, or only suggest?
